### Business Insights

1. **Customer tenure:** Customers with shorter tenure may require additional onboarding and retention support, as longer tenure is associated with lower churn odds in our Logistic Regression model.

2. **Contract type:** Customers with month-to-month contracts may be a priority for retention campaigns. The company could investigate whether suitable long-term plans and incentives improve retention.

3. **Internet service:** Fiber-optic customers show a positive churn coefficient in our model. The company should investigate possible pricing, service quality, and customer satisfaction issues.

4. **Retention strategy:** The model can help identify customers who may be at higher risk of leaving. The company can prioritize these customers for personalized offers and support.

5. **Limitations:** These findings represent associations learned from historical data and do not establish causation. The effectiveness of retention strategies should be validated through further analysis and controlled experiments.
